Fallible conversion from u32 to char, const equivalent of char::from_u32

§Const stabilization

The equivalent std function was const-stabilized in Rust 1.67.0.

§Example

use konst::{chr, option};

const AT: char = option::unwrap!(chr::from_u32(64));

assert_eq!(AT, '@');
