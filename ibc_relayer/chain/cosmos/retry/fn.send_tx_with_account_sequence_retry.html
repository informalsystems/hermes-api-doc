Expand description

Try to send_tx and retry on account sequence error with re-cached account s.n. An account sequence error can occur if the account sequence that the relayer caches becomes outdated.

Account sequence mismatch error can occur at two separate steps:

  1. as Err variant, propagated from the estimate_gas step.
  2. as an Ok variant, with an Code::Err response, propagated from the broadcast_tx_sync step.

We treat both cases by re-fetching the account sequence number from the full node and retrying once with the new account s.n.
